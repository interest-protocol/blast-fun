# Grill Me and explainable discovery

- Status: candidate direction; not approved for implementation
- Captured: 2026-08-23

## Thesis

> The launchpad where every coin has to explain itself.

Fast creation, bonding curves, DEX graduation, creator fees, and referrals are
category requirements. Blast's candidate advantage is intelligibility:
promotion has visible reasons, claims have explicit evidence states, and one
launch history survives the full lifecycle.

This direction preserves a permissionless protocol. It adds neither launch
approval nor an on-chain quality score.

## Candidate product

- **Helmet Check:** generate a short adversarial interview from launch
  configuration and observable facts. Record answers, edits, omissions, and
  challenges. Label each claim as verified, asserted, or unknown; AI asks and
  summarizes but never endorses.
- **Explainable discovery:** every ranking answers why it appears. Favor sustained
  participation, concentration changes, liquidity, lifecycle, identity, and
  response history over raw activity. Ranking belongs in the indexer and
  application layer.
- **Flight Recorder:** keep one URL through New, Bonding, Migration, and Bonded.
  Preserve the initial thesis, parameters, identity history, risk context,
  migration outcome, and later updates.
- **Reputation:** show outcomes and response history rather than generic badges.
  Any curator reward follows an observation window and disclosed position.
- **Distribution lanes:** pair ecosystem promotion with quote liquidity,
  issuer/control context, and the effect on graduation value.

## Boundaries

- Discovery may be opinionated only when its reasons are visible.
- Verification names the fact proved; it never implies safety.
- Grill Me is a public due-diligence record, not an approval gate.
- Start with one understandable curve before adding economic modes.
- Treat graduation as a lifecycle transition, not the end of the launch page.

Deprioritize launch-time livestreaming, raw activity points, and secret AI
scores. They add moderation, sybil, wash-trading, or trust costs before the core
lifecycle is validated.

## Validation before protocol work

Prototype the read-only experience on five representative launches. Compare
conventional Trending cards with cards that expose their ranking inputs. Test
whether users can identify creator, fee, quote-asset, and migration risks, then
measure qualified token-page opens and unique-wallet conversion.

Protocol scope remains unchanged until users understand the named risks better
and explainable cards improve qualified engagement without a financial
recommendation.
